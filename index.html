01 · about

From analytics to
intelligent systems

My story starts with data, not models. At Allen Classes I spent two years turning attendance registers, onboarding funnels and loyalty tiers into dashboards that leadership across Bihar actually made decisions from. At IDFC First Bank I moved from describing the past to predicting it — modelling seasonality and campaign impact closely enough to forecast support demand with 90%+ accuracy and cut overstaffing costs by 15%. That period taught me the thing I still lead with: a number only matters when someone can act on it.

Then the models got interesting. I went to IIT Patna for an M.Tech in AI & Data Science, where my thesis grew into a RAG chatbot that let students ask institute documents questions in plain language — and where one query-classification layer cut tokens per chat by 40%.

But a model in a notebook helps nobody. So I learned to ship the whole thing — React on the front, FastAPI and Node/Express behind it, PostgreSQL, MongoDB and ChromaDB underneath, Docker and AWS around it. That's how an AI-enabled resort ERP went from idea to a live product running bookings, billing, QR ordering and staff management, with CLIP-powered ID verification that cut check-in time by 90%. Data, the model, and the product around it — I like owning all three.
